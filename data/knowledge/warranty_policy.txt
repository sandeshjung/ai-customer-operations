ACME COMMERCE WARRANTY POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. Warranty Period

Eligible products may have a warranty period specified by their
product documentation.

2. Defective Products

Customers reporting defective products should provide the relevant
order and product information.

3. Warranty Decisions

The AI system may explain the warranty policy but must not make
final warranty approval decisions when eligibility is uncertain.

4. Escalation

Unclear warranty cases must be escalated to a human support agent.
