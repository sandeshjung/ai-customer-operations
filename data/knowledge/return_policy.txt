# Product Return Policy

**Policy ID:** RET-001
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Customer Experience
**Review Frequency:** Semiannual

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for returning purchased products and receiving an eligible refund or replacement.

---

## 2. Standard Return Window

Eligible products may generally be returned within **30 calendar days of confirmed delivery**.

The return period begins on the date the order is recorded as delivered.

For orders with multiple products delivered separately, the return period applies independently to each delivered product.

---

## 3. Return Eligibility

A product may be eligible for return when:

* It was purchased through an authorized sales channel.
* The return request is within the applicable return period.
* The product is eligible for return.
* Required product information can be verified.

Some product categories may have additional restrictions.

---

## 4. Product Condition

Returned products should generally be:

* In reasonable condition.
* Accompanied by required accessories where applicable.
* Accompanied by documentation where required.
* Packaged appropriately for return shipping.

Products that show substantial damage caused after delivery may require additional investigation.

---

## 5. Defective Products

Defective products may be handled under the warranty process rather than the standard return process.

If a customer reports a defect:

1. Verify the product.
2. Determine whether the issue appears to be a manufacturing defect.
3. Check warranty eligibility.
4. Follow the warranty procedure when applicable.

The agent should not automatically classify every product complaint as a warranty claim.

---

## 6. Non-Returnable Products

Certain categories may have return restrictions for operational, legal, hygiene, personalization, or safety reasons.

When a product appears to be restricted:

* Check the applicable product category policy.
* Do not promise that the item can be returned.
* Escalate ambiguous cases.

---

## 7. Return Shipping

Responsibility for return shipping may depend on the reason for the return.

Examples include:

* Incorrect product shipped.
* Defective product.
* Customer preference.
* Product not as described.
* Other approved exception.

The applicable policy should be checked before promising reimbursement of return shipping costs.

---

## 8. Refund After Return

Receiving a returned package does not necessarily mean that a refund has already been approved.

The returned product may require inspection or verification.

Support should distinguish between:

* Return initiated.
* Return received.
* Return inspected.
* Refund approved.
* Refund processed.

---

## 9. Exceptions

Customers may request an exception when:

* The return window has recently expired.
* The customer experienced a documented delivery issue.
* The product has a verified defect.
* An operational error caused the customer to miss the normal return process.

Exceptions require human review.

---

## 10. Automated Agent Restrictions

The automated agent may explain eligibility requirements and recommend next steps.

It must not:

* Guarantee approval.
* Override the return window.
* Approve an exception.
* Promise reimbursement.
* Modify return records.

---

## 11. Escalation

Human review is required when:

* The return window has expired.
* Product eligibility is unclear.
* A customer requests an exception.
* Product condition is disputed.
* A return appears fraudulent or inconsistent.
* The applicable product category has conflicting rules.

---

## 12. Decision Principle

The system should distinguish between **return eligibility** and **refund approval**.

A customer may be eligible to initiate a return without an immediate refund being guaranteed.
