ACME COMMERCE SHIPPING POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. Standard Delivery

Standard delivery normally takes 3–5 business days.

2. Delayed Shipments

An order is considered delayed when the current date is later than
the expected delivery date.

If an order is delayed by more than 48 hours:

- The customer should be notified.
- The shipment tracking information should be reviewed.
- If tracking has not updated for more than 48 hours, the carrier
  should be contacted.

3. Serious Delays

If an order is delayed by more than 5 days:

- The case should be treated as high priority.
- The shipment should be investigated.
- A support ticket should be created.
- The customer should be notified.

4. Missing Tracking Information

If an order is delayed but has no tracking number:

- Do not assume the shipment was lost.
- Verify the order and shipment records.
- Escalate the case to a support agent.

5. Lost Packages

A package may be considered potentially lost when:

- The carrier confirms loss, OR
- Tracking has shown no movement for 7 or more days.

The AI agent must not independently declare a package lost without
carrier confirmation.

6. Customer Communication

Customers should receive accurate information based on verified
order and shipment data.

The system must not invent tracking locations, carrier updates,
delivery dates, or refund decisions.
