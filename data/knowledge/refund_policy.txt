ACME COMMERCE REFUND POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. General Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the order status and reason for the
refund request.

2. Delayed Orders

A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund.

The support team must review the order and shipment circumstances
before approving a refund.

3. Cancelled Orders

If an order has not shipped and cancellation is approved, the
customer may be eligible for a full refund.

4. Delivered Orders

Refund requests for delivered orders require review according to
the return and refund procedures.

5. AI Restrictions

The AI agent must not promise a refund unless the applicable policy
and order information clearly support the action.

When eligibility is unclear, escalate to a human support agent.
