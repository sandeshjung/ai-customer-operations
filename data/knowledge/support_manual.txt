ACME COMMERCE SUPPORT MANUAL
Version: 1.0

1. General Rule

Support decisions must be based on verified customer, order,
shipment, and policy information.

2. Missing Information

If important information is missing, the AI agent should retrieve
additional information or escalate the case.

3. High Priority Cases

Cases involving serious shipment delays, potentially lost packages,
or unclear customer-impacting decisions should receive higher
priority.

4. Human Escalation

The AI should escalate when:

- Required information is unavailable.
- Policy interpretation is ambiguous.
- The requested action requires human authorization.
- The customer situation is unusually complex.

5. Customer Communication

Messages must be factual, concise, and based on verified
information.

Never invent:

- tracking information
- delivery dates
- refund approvals
- carrier actions
- policy exceptions
